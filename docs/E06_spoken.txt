# --- 1 · HOOK ---
So far, every sum we've done has used one rod.
Today, we're using two and it's easier than it sounds.

# --- 2 · WHAT THE SECOND ROD IS FOR ---
Remember, each rod has its own place.
The rod on the right is the ones rod.
The rod next to it is the tens rod.
Together, they can show every number up to ninety-nine.

# --- 3 · THE BIG IDEA, and both ordering rules ---
Here's the whole trick for today.
We never work on two rods at the same time.
We do one rod, then the other rod.
And we always start on the right, with the ones rod.
On each rod, the lower beads move first, then the upper bead.
Each rod is just a small sum on its own.

# --- 4 · TWENTY-ONE PLUS THREE ---
Let's try twenty-one plus three.
First, make twenty-one.
Push one lower bead up on the ones rod.
Push two lower beads up on the tens rod.
The abacus is showing twenty-one.
Now we add three.
Three is a ones number, so only the ones rod moves.
Push three more lower beads up on the ones rod.
The tens rod doesn't move at all.
It's still showing twenty.
And the ones rod is showing four.
So twenty-one plus three is twenty-four.

# --- 5 · THIRTEEN PLUS TWENTY-SIX ---
Now let's move both rods.
Let's try thirteen plus twenty-six.
First, make thirteen.
Push three lower beads up on the ones rod.
Push one lower bead up on the tens rod.
Now we add twenty-six.
Twenty-six is twenty.
And six.
We always start with the ones rod.
Six is five.
And one.
Lower beads always move first.
So push one lower bead up.
Then push the upper bead down for five.
The ones rod is showing nine.
Now the tens rod.
Twenty means two more tens.
Push two more lower beads up on the tens rod.
The tens rod is showing three.
Three tens and nine ones.
So thirteen plus twenty-six is thirty-nine.

# --- 6 · THE RULE SO FAR ---
Did you see what we did?
Two little sums, one after the other.
That's all a big sum ever is.

# --- 7 · THIRTY-NINE TAKE AWAY FIFTEEN ---
Taking away works the same way.
Let's try thirty-nine take away fifteen.
Our abacus is already showing thirty-nine.
Fifteen is ten.
And five.
Start with the ones rod.
We need to take away five.
Five is the upper bead.
So push it back up.
The four lower beads stay where they are.
Now the tens rod.
Ten means one ten goes away.
Push one lower bead down on the tens rod.
The tens rod is showing two.
Two tens and four ones.
So thirty-nine take away fifteen is twenty-four.

# --- 8 · YOUR TURN ---
Now it's your turn.
Make twenty-three on your own abacus.
Then add eleven.
Remember the ones rod first.
What number is showing now?
Twenty-three plus eleven is thirty-four.
Great job!
