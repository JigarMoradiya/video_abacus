# THE HIGHEST-HOOK EPISODE IN THE SERIES. Everything before this has been "here is how the beads
# work". This is the first one that says "here is something you cannot do — and here is the trick".
# It is the video most likely to be watched by a parent who is deciding whether this is worth their
# child's time, so it has to land.
#
# E06's teaser promised "a sum the beads cannot do, and the clever trick that fixes it". This pays
# both halves. A video that poses the problem and does not solve it would be a broken promise.
#
# ONE ROD ONLY. Nothing here touches the tens rod — the trick being taught is the SMALL FRIEND
# (the 5-complement), and dragging in a second rod would blur what is actually new.
#
# THE ORDER RULES FROM E06 STILL HOLD, and this episode depends on the second one:
#   +3 = take away 2, then push the upper bead down.
# Lower beads move first, then the upper bead — which is why E06 taught that rule before this.
#
# The three worked sums, all genuinely blocked, all checked digit by digit:
#   3 + 3 = 6   take 2 away, add 5
#   4 + 3 = 7   take 2 away, add 5
#   2 + 4 = 6   take 1 away, add 5   (your turn)
#
# ⏸ HOLD THREE SECONDS after "What number is showing now?"


# --- 1 · THE HOOK: a sum that gets stuck ---
Today we are going to get stuck.
And then we are going to learn a trick.
Let us try three plus three.

# --- 2 · IT GOES WRONG ---
First, make three.
Push three lower beads up on the ones rod.
Now we want to add three more.
So push one more bead up.
And another one.
And now we are stuck.
There is no bead left to push.
Count them.
One, two, three, four.
Four is all the lower beads a rod has.
So we cannot add three the easy way.

# --- 3 · THE THING WE FORGOT ---
But look up here.
The upper bead has not moved at all.
And the upper bead is worth five.
Five is bigger than three.
So we have more than enough.
We just have to use it cleverly.

# --- 4 · FRIENDS OF FIVE ---
Here is the clever part.
Every small number has a friend.
Two friends that make five together.
One and four are friends.
Two and three are friends.
Say them with me.
One and four.
Two and three.
These are called the small friends.

# --- 5 · THE TRICK ---
Now watch what the friends do.
We wanted to add three.
Three's friend is two.
So instead of adding three, we add five and take away two.
Because five take away two is three.
It is the same answer, the easy way round.

# --- 6 · THE TRICK ON THE ABACUS ---
Let us do it.
Our rod is showing three.
Remember, lower beads move first.
So take away two lower beads.
Now the rod shows one.
Then push the upper bead down for five.
And now the rod shows six.
Three plus three is six.
We did it, and we never ran out of beads.

# --- 7 · ONE MORE ---
Let us try four plus three.
First, make four.
Push all four lower beads up.
Now add three.
Three's friend is two again.
So take away two lower beads.
The rod shows two.
Then push the upper bead down.
The rod shows seven.
Four plus three is seven.

# --- 8 · YOUR TURN ---
Now it is your turn.
Make two on your own abacus.
Then add four.
Four's friend is one.
So take away one, and push the upper bead down.
What number is showing now?
Two plus four is six.
Well done.

That is your first abacus formula.
There is one for every small number, and we will learn them all.
