# Quick Subtraction on Rod 1 — recording script (9:16 short reel, ~45-50s)
# Lines starting with # are notes and are NOT read aloud. Everything else IS read aloud,
# exactly as written.
#
# Revised: the first draft only ever removed lower beads OR the upper bead alone, never
# both together — user caught that gap ("what about 8-6 or 9-6?"). Two of the four examples
# below now genuinely move both bead types in the SAME action. All four are still "it just
# fits" — no complement/friend trick, because that trick is exactly what the Math Heroes
# reels exist to pay off:
#
#   3 - 2 = 1   lower beads only
#   7 - 5 = 2   upper bead only, lower beads untouched
#   9 - 6 = 3   upper bead AND one lower bead together (5+1=6)
#   8 - 7 = 1   upper bead AND two lower beads together (5+2=7)
#
# Both compound examples move lower beads first, then the upper bead — same order as the
# tip line states, and the same order reel_quicksum_lines.txt (Adding) uses.
#
# Between each example the rod resets to empty before the next one starts — the short
# transition line is the beat where that reset happens on screen.

Taking away on one rod is super quick — check out these examples!
Three beads are up. Pull two down — three take away two is one!
Let's try another!
Two lower beads, plus the upper bead — that's seven. Lift the upper bead — seven take away five is two!
Next one!
Four lower beads, plus the upper bead — that's nine. Pull one lower bead down, and lift the upper bead — nine take away six is three!
One more!
Three lower beads, plus the upper bead — that's eight. Pull two lower beads down, and lift the upper bead — eight take away seven is one!
Here's one tip: lower beads always move first, then the upper bead!
Lower beads or the upper bead — taking away is always quick!
