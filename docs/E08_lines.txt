# E08 · All four small friends — narration, one continuous take.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored by the aligner. Do not read them.
#
# E07's take ends "Next time, we will learn all four small friends for adding." This pays it.
#
# ═══ READ THIS BEFORE RECORDING — the voice of this episode ═══
#
# TEACH, DO NOT READ. The first draft of this script was correct and lifeless: eighty-seven
# statements in a row, every one delivered at the same pitch. A real teacher does four things a
# narrator never does, and all four are built into the lines below:
#
#   1. ASKS, then WAITS. Every ⏸ is a real silence. Do not fill it. The child is answering.
#      Then say their answer back to them as if you heard it — "Four. Well done!"
#   2. REACTS. "Oh no. Zero?" is not information, it is a face. Play the worry, then the rescue.
#   3. TALKS TO ONE CHILD. "You tell me", "your abacus", "you are getting fast at this."
#      Never "we can observe that". One child, sitting next to you.
#   4. CHANGES SPEED. Slow and careful on a new rule. Quick and light on "Done!" and "Friends!"
#      A flat pace is what made the first draft sound like a manual.
#
# The ⏸ marks are part of the script. There are eight. If they are read through, the episode
# becomes a lecture again no matter how warmly the lines are said.
#
# ═══ CONTENT ═══
#
# What is actually new here is not "three more formulas" — E07 taught the trick but never taught
# WHEN to use it. A child who takes E07 literally will use the friend on two plus two. So the
# spine is the TRIGGER (section 3: look at the lower beads first) and the friends hang off it.
#
# ONE ROD ONLY, same as E07. Every result stays under ten, so nothing touches the tens rod.
# THE ORDER RULE FROM E06 STILL HOLDS: lower beads move first, then the upper bead.
#
# The worked sums, every one checked digit by digit:
#   2 + 2 = 4   DIRECT — the contrast case, no trick, this is the point of section 3
#   4 + 1 = 5   friend 4   take away four, then five     WE SHOW
#   4 + 2 = 6   friend 3   take away three, then five    WE SHOW
#   3 + 4 = 7   friend 1   take away one, then five      WE SHOW
#   2 + 3 = 5   friend 2   take away two, then five      THEY DO
#
# ALL FOUR FORMULAS ARE COVERED, and +3 is the one the child does alone. That is deliberate and it
# is the right way round: +3 is the formula E07 already taught, so the your-turn asks them to use
# something they OWN rather than something they watched once four lines ago.
#
# NOTATION: `+n = −(5−n) +5` — FRIEND FIRST, then five. This is a correction from the first draft,
# which wrote it `+n = +5 − (5−n)` and then said "add five, take away four" in the four-tricks
# recap — five-first in speech and in symbols, while every worked example in sections 4-8 said
# "take away four, and add five" — friend-first, matching the bead order (lower beads move first,
# then the upper bead, per E06's rule). Two different orders for one idea in the same script is
# exactly the kind of inconsistency that makes a formula feel arbitrary instead of learnable.
# FRIEND FIRST IS WHAT THE HANDS ACTUALLY DO, so it is what BOTH the speech and the symbols say
# from here on — in this script, and in the card/board text that renders it.


# --- 1 · RECAP HOOK ---
Hello again!
Last time, we got stuck.
Do you remember what saved us?
# ⏸ hold 2s — let them answer
The trick!
Take away a friend, and add five.
Today we learn all four.
Ready? Let us go.


# --- 2 · MEET THE FRIENDS ---
First, meet the friends.
Every small number has one.
Two friends that make five together.
Look. One and four.
One and four make five.
So they are friends.
And two and three.
Two and three make five as well.
Friends!
And that is all of them. Just two pairs.
Now you tell me.
Who is one's friend?
# ⏸ hold 2s
Four. Well done!
And two's friend?
# ⏸ hold 2s
Three. You have got it.
So three's friend is two.
And four's friend is one.

# The four formulas. Line 4 of these is DELIBERATELY handed to the child — a teacher stops
# reciting the moment the pattern is guessable, and lets them finish it.
Now watch what the friends do for us.
To add one, take away four and add five.
To add two, take away three and add five.
To add three, take away two and add five.
Now you guess the last one.
# ⏸ hold 3s — the biggest pause in the episode
To add four, take away one and add five.
Did you spot it?
The number we take away is always the friend.
Every single time.


# --- 3 · WHEN DO WE NEED IT? ---
Now, careful here. This part is important.
We do not always need a trick.
Always look at the lower beads first.
If there are enough, just push them up.
Watch. Two plus two.
Push two lower beads up.
Now push two more.
Done!
That was easy. No trick at all.
But now try four plus one.
Push all four lower beads up.
We want just one more.
Have a look. Is there a bead left?
# ⏸ hold 2s
No. Not one.
And that is when we call a friend.


# --- 4 · ADD ONE ---
So. One's friend is four.
That means take away four, and add five.
Remember our rule. Lower beads move first.
So down they come. All four of them.
Now the rod shows zero.
Oh no. Zero?
Do not worry. Watch this.
Push the upper bead down.
And there it is. Five!
Four plus one is five.
We wanted one more, and we got it.


# --- 5 · ADD TWO ---
Your turn to think. Four plus two.
Clear the rod, and make four again.
Now, who is two's friend?
# ⏸ hold 2s
Three!
So take away three, and add five.
Down go three lower beads.
The rod shows one.
Now bring the upper bead down.
Six! Four plus two is six.


# --- 6 · ADD FOUR ---
One more. Three plus four.
Clear the rod.
Push three lower beads up.
Now we add four.
And four's friend is...
# ⏸ hold 2s
One!
So take away one bead.
The rod shows two.
Bring the upper bead down.
Seven! Three plus four is seven.
You are getting fast at this.

# --- THE LIKE ASK — MID-ROLL, NOT AT THE END ---

# Here it lands on the praise beat, right after the child's third win — which is what the rule
# always MEANT by "while the viewer is feeling good". Keep it to two lines and keep it quick;
# a long ask in the middle costs more momentum than it earns.

# --- 7 · THE PATTERN ---
Now stop and look.
Did you see it?
Every single time, we did the same two things.
Take away the friend.
Then bring the upper bead down.
That is all.
The friend changes.
But the five never does.


# --- 8 · YOUR TURN ---
Now it is your turn. All on your own.
Make two on your abacus.
Then add three.
Think. What is three's friend?
Take it away, then bring the upper bead down.
What number is showing now?
# ⏸ hold 3s — the recall gap, same as E07
Two plus three is five.
Did you get it? Well done!
That was the fourth friend, and you did it by yourself.

So now you know all four small friends for adding.
That is a real abacus formula, and it is yours.
