# E06 · Two rods together — narration, one continuous take.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored by the aligner. Do not read them.
#
# Direct addition AND subtraction across the ones and tens rods. Nothing here needs a complement:
# every single digit stays inside its own rod, which is what makes this the last episode before the
# formulas. Source: the app's Level 2 chapters 1-4 extended to two columns, plus Level 1 place value.
#
# THE HOOK IS "ONE ROD AT A TIME". Two rods look like twice the work and are not — they are the same
# work done twice, and that is the whole lesson.
#
# TWO ORDERING RULES, both stated AND demonstrated:
#   1. RIGHT TO LEFT — the ones rod first, then the tens.
#   2. WITHIN A ROD — the lower beads first, then the upper bead.
#
# Rule 2 needed somewhere to happen. The middle sum was fourteen plus twenty-five, whose ones rod
# only ever moves the upper bead, so the rule would have been asserted and never shown. Thirteen plus
# twenty-six needs BOTH on the ones rod (six is five and one), which is also where a child meets the
# decomposition that every formula from E08 on depends on. It still lands on thirty-nine, so the
# subtraction is still thirty-nine take away fifteen — the sum E05's teaser promised.
#
# THE ORDER IS RIGHT TO LEFT: ones rod first, then tens. Stated as a rule, and demonstrated that way
# EVERYWHERE — including when a number is merely being SET UP, not calculated.
#
# The first pass at this fixed the two sums and left "make fourteen" starting on the tens rod, two
# lines after the script had just said we always start on the right. A rule the very next instruction
# breaks is worse than no rule: the child follows what they are shown.
#
# v1 did the tens first and only declined to call it a law — which taught it anyway, because a child
# copies the worked example, not the sentence the script chose not to write. It also breaks the
# episodes after this one: big friend carries FROM the ones INTO the tens, so a child who finishes the
# tens first has to go back to a rod they were told was done.
#
# E05's teaser promised twenty-one plus three and thirty-nine take away fifteen. Both are here,
# and both answers are twenty-four.
#
# ⏸ HOLD THREE SECONDS after "What number is showing now?"


# --- 1 · HOOK ---
So far, every sum we have done has used one rod.
Today we are using two.
And it is easier than it sounds.

# --- 2 · WHAT THE SECOND ROD IS FOR ---
Remember, each rod has its own place.
The rod on the right is the ones.
The rod next to it is the tens.
Together they can show every number up to ninety-nine.

# --- 3 · THE BIG IDEA ---
Here is the whole trick for today.
We never work on two rods at the same time.
We do one rod, then the other rod.
And we always start on the right, with the ones rod.
On each rod, the lower beads move first, then the upper bead.
Each rod is just a small sum on its own.

# --- 4 · TWENTY-ONE PLUS THREE ---
Let us try twenty-one plus three.
First, make twenty-one.
Push one lower bead up on the ones rod.
Push two lower beads up on the tens rod.
The abacus is showing twenty-one.
Now we add three.
Three is a ones number, so only the ones rod moves.
Push three more lower beads up on the ones rod.
The tens rod does not move at all.
It is still showing twenty.
And the ones rod is showing four.
So twenty-one plus three is twenty-four.

# --- 5 · THIRTEEN PLUS TWENTY-SIX ---
Now let us move both rods.
Let us try thirteen plus twenty-six.
First, make thirteen.
Three lower beads up on the ones rod.
One lower bead up on the tens rod.
Now we add twenty-six.
Twenty-six is twenty and six.
We always start with the ones rod.
Six is five and one.
Lower beads always move first.
So push one lower bead up.
Then push the upper bead down for five.
The ones rod is showing nine.
Now the tens rod.
Twenty means two more tens.
Push two more lower beads up on the tens rod.
The tens rod is showing three.
Three tens and nine ones.
So thirteen plus twenty-six is thirty-nine.

# --- 6 · THE RULE SO FAR ---
Did you see what we did?
Two little sums, one after the other.
That is all a big sum ever is.

# --- 7 · THIRTY-NINE TAKE AWAY FIFTEEN ---
Taking away works the same way.
Let us try thirty-nine take away fifteen.
Our abacus is already showing thirty-nine.
Fifteen is ten and five.
Start with the ones rod again.
We need to take away five.
Five is the upper bead, so push it back up.
The four lower beads stay where they are.
Now the tens rod.
Ten means one ten goes away.
Push one lower bead down on the tens rod.
The tens rod is showing two.
Two tens and four ones.
So thirty-nine take away fifteen is twenty-four.

# --- 8 · YOUR TURN ---
Now it is your turn.
Make twenty-three on your own abacus.
Then add eleven.
Remember, the ones rod first.
What number is showing now?
Twenty-three plus eleven is thirty-four.
Well done.
