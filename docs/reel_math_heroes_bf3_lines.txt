# THIS SHORT'S HOOK: The prize wall is almost empty and a stuck number blocks the last prize.
# Alert/celebration lines carry the setting; every math line is plain wording ("stuck" / "not
# enough to take away" / "add ___, then take away ten"). Alert speaker alternates by slot.

# FORMULA SPLIT (revision 9): BIG FRIEND TAKING AWAY, random half of the eight non-self-paired
# subtract formulas: −1, −3, −6, −8 (a mix of small and big numbers; the other half, −2, −4, −7, −9,
# is short 4; −5 is in short 6). Friend-first: −n = +friend − 10.
#
#   Mission 1:  85 − 6   friend 4   -> 79
#   Mission 2:  60 − 1   friend 9   -> 59
#   Mission 3:  96 − 8   friend 2   -> 88
#   Mission 4:  72 − 3   friend 7   -> 69
