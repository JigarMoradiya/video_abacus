# Math Heroes 2 — recording script (9:16 short reel, ~40-45s)
# Lines starting with # are notes and are NOT read aloud — same rule the full episode scripts
# use. Everything else IS read aloud, exactly as written.
#
# Two voices: NARRATOR and BLAZE and SPARK. A "# BOTH" line means Blaze and Spark read that one
# line together (or, if you only have one voice, just read it once yourself — it still works).
# Read at a quick, energetic pace — this is a short-form hook, not a slow lesson.
#
# THE TRICK THIS TIME: Small Friend SUBTRACTION — the mirror of reel 1's addition trick.
# Same "friend" idea (a number's friend is what it takes to make 5), and the SAME bead order
# as reel 1: the friend moves first, five moves second. Reel 1 taught the friends of 2 and 1;
# this one teaches the friends of 1 and 3 — together the two reels cover all four.

# NARRATOR
When a number gets stuck, Math Heroes save the day!

# BLAZE
Alert! Five take away one, and it's stuck!

# SPARK
There's no room to take away one. Let's call a friend!

# hold silent ~3s here for the mission-timer countdown (3, 2, 1) — nothing spoken

# SPARK
One's friend is four!

# BLAZE
Add four, then take away five!

# BOTH
Five take away one is four! Mission solved!

# BLAZE
Another alert! Five take away three, and it's stuck!

# SPARK
There's no room to take away three. Let's call a friend again!

# hold silent ~3s here for the mission-timer countdown (3, 2, 1) — nothing spoken

# SPARK
Three's friend is two!

# BLAZE
Add two, then take away five!

# BOTH
Five take away three is two! Heroes win again!

# NARRATOR
Every stuck number has a trick. Learn them all with Abacus Kids — free on the App Store and Google Play!
