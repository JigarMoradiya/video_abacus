# Script checklist — run this BEFORE showing any script, and again before recording

Every rule the user has given across E11 to E15 and the Math Heroes shorts, in one place. Sources:
`EPISODE_RULES.md`, the E11 to E15 script headers, `reel_math_heroes_bf_PLAN.txt` (revisions 1 to 10),
and the corrections made along the way. Tick every box on the whole script, line by line, before
reporting. Do not fix only what was pointed at: fix this whole list in one pass.

## 1. Intro and hook
- [ ] The hook echoes the previous episode's teaser line word for word.
- [ ] The first lines say WHAT today is about and WHY we need it, in kid words. A viewer who lands cold
      must know in the first 10 seconds.
- [ ] NEED comes before NAME: state the problem, then the helper, then the name ("family"), never the
      name first.
- [ ] A short reminder of the earlier trick (a callback), not a re-teach.
- [ ] One line saying WHEN to look for the trick ("look at the ones rod first: ...").
- [ ] The wall (stuck moment) in the first example proves the problem already told. Do not explain it
      twice word for word; the second time is a short callback.
- [ ] Short reels: the first words of the opening line name the math/trick, story setting second;
      each short stands alone, so it introduces the trick itself (what it is and why).

## 2. Teaching order
- [ ] The FORMULA is introduced and explained BEFORE any bead moves, then the beads, then the answer.
- [ ] Formula is spoken friend-first with the right n and the right friend, the way E12/E13 say it
      ("Plus six equals plus one, minus five, plus ten.").
- [ ] Derivation is short and true (for Family: plus ten adds four too many, minus four is plus one,
      minus five).
- [ ] Shape: recap, hook, why, formula, worked examples, pattern recap, your turn, close with store CTA
      and the next-episode teaser.
- [ ] Nothing that is only true for some numbers is taught as a rule for all numbers.
- [ ] Never say "subtraction" or "negative": say "taking away".

## 3. Bead and finger wording
- [ ] Every example has a "Make <number>" line AND a friend line ("Six's friend is four").
- [ ] Making a number: ONES rod first, then the tens rod, and on every rod lower beads before the
      upper bead ("one lower bead on the ones rod, and one lower bead on the tens rod"). Say which rod
      for a single digit too ("on the ones rod"). This is the user's rule: ones rod first always, in
      moves and in makeup.
- [ ] Number makeup: LOWER beads before the UPPER bead, always ("two lower beads, and one upper bead").
- [ ] Moves: ones rod first, lower beads before the upper bead, tens rod last.
- [ ] Finger is decided by DIRECTION, never by bead: UP is the thumb, DOWN is the index finger
      (E01 wording: "push them up with your thumb").
- [ ] Finger hint at the FIRST move of the first example (clear, with a hold after it), light
      reminders later ("Thumb again!"), a reminder in the pattern recap, and one in your turn BEFORE the
      viewer moves, without giving the answer.
- [ ] The finger rule covers the "Make <number>" setup moves too, not only the formula moves: making a
      number pushes lower beads UP (thumb) and the upper bead DOWN (index finger), so the index finger
      must be heard at least once in the first example, and the rule said once in a kid sentence
      ("up is the thumb, and down is the index finger").
- [ ] Do not narrate the in-between number mid-move unless it is the teaching point.
- [ ] Each move gets its own line (one visual change per line), with a 1 to 2 second hold between
      steps.

## 4. Examples and math checks (every example, show the table)
- [ ] ONES rod: the trick trigger really holds, and every sub-step is a legal physical move.
- [ ] Right trick. Family PLUS (adding): ones digit d is 5 or more and the friend is more than the lower
      beads showing (+6 d 5-8, +7 d 5-7, +8 d 5-6, +9 d 5). Family MINUS (taking away): d is below 5
      and 4 - d < friend <= 4 (-6 d 1-4, -7 d 2-4, -8 d 3-4, -9 d 4). Plain Big Friend: the friend goes
      on/off directly. Family cases never hide in Big Friend content and the reverse. Family PLUS moves
      are all UP (thumb); Family MINUS moves are all DOWN (index finger); number makeup is always the
      usual mix.
- [ ] TENS rod: the carry/borrow is ONE plain bead. Add: tens digit NOT 4 (and not 9). Take away:
      tens digit NOT 5 (and not 0).
- [ ] No 50-shaped numbers (no start or result of 50).
- [ ] All numbers distinct across the whole script, no repeated example.
- [ ] Varied: first examples may be one-digit plus one-digit teens (tens digit 0), then one or two
      two-digit numbers. Shorts: random formula mix and random order, no ascending or descending runs,
      not a low group and a high group.
- [ ] The numbers in the text match the table, the formula and the beads (re-add every one).
- [ ] Complete friend pair lists when a list is spoken: nine and one, eight and two, seven and three,
      six and four, five and five.
- [ ] A question NEVER states its own answer, and the formula card/total appear on the answer line,
      not on the question.

## 5. Pacing and pauses
- [ ] Reading note at the top of the expressive file: every [pause N seconds] is a REAL held silence.
- [ ] 3 second hold after every question; 1 to 2 seconds between steps; extra hold after a formula
      card or a reveal; 3 second recall hold after "What number is showing now?".
- [ ] Runtime target about 5 to 6 minutes. Calibrate: E13 was 673 words in 351 seconds (about 0.49
      seconds per word, holds included). Count words and holds and report the estimate.
- [ ] Short reels: 4 missions with a 3 second countdown hold each; report the runtime.

## 6. Language
- [ ] Easy words a 6 to 8 year old owns; short sentences; one idea per line; no jargon.
- [ ] Math-first: every line carries math or the next step; story is only flavor.
- [ ] Vocabulary matches the app (rods, upper/lower beads, ones/tens rod, "Family").
- [ ] Numbers spelled as words; no stray digits in the spoken text.
- [ ] Read the whole script once as a 6 to 8 year old would hear it: any sentence that needs a second
      sentence to explain it was wrong.
- [ ] No repeated lines or word-for-word repeats of the same explanation.

## 7. Structure and callbacks
- [ ] New world and new mascot pair/family (check theme.ts and the components; no repeats).
- [ ] Like-ask in a NEW spot, right after a win, two lines, new wording, logged in EPISODE_RULES 3.9a;
      never in the close; includes a subscribe beat in the middle of the episode.
- [ ] Callbacks to the previous episodes.
- [ ] Close: practice prompt, the store CTA naming both stores with new wording, the teaser for the
      next episode, no promise of a schedule.
- [ ] Plain file and expressive file match exactly once tags and pauses are stripped (run the diff).
- [ ] Plain header carries the resolved facts, the formula table, the example verification table, the
      world/mascot brief, the like-ask spot and the finger-hint spots.
- [ ] Check the app (iOS first) for the real definition before writing, and say which doc was wrong.

## 8. Build and recording stage
- [ ] Highlights, flashes and cards follow the exact words the line is saying (word-synced).
- [ ] Check the RECORDED words against the script (mis-reads, a changed number).
- [ ] Check splice points for clipped word endings and clicks: use silence detection and a
      spectrogram, not only volume.
- [ ] Hold the pauses at full length when recording; do not stretch them afterwards.
- [ ] FOCUS (build stage): ask "what do I see first?" for EVERY phrase, and the answer must be the lesson part
      (abacus, friend pair, formula, answer). Background steps back (desaturate + soft wash ~35-50%, quieter
      bunting/balloons/rays); the lesson parts are large, with a clean light card, a bright rim and a shadow;
      characters and the machine shrink/calm and move to the edges during teaching beats and come forward
      (full colour, full energy) for the alert, the celebration and the finale; fewer simultaneous elements
      (one headline + one focus object + quiet characters). Drive it from ONE eased split value per frame
      (pure function of the frame, no pops), and assert the interpolated layout boxes every frame.
      Prefer calming (softer, smaller, further back) over deleting the fun. bf1 is the reference build.
- [ ] Text tags/signs have even left/right padding (at least ~28 px each side) sized to the LONGEST variant of the
      text (every digit and word variant checked in a still), not a fixed width that only fits the shortest.
- [ ] Guards: no overlapping boxes, nothing off the frame; render stills for every phrase; check both
      cuts (16:9 and 4:5) and the thumbnails.
- Safe area = small margins only (SAFE: left/right 48, top 56, bottom 1860) so nothing touches the extreme edges; the caption stays at the bottom like the other shorts, logo top-right, powered-by bottom-left; no empty bands (the lesson part runs down to just above the caption). Question headers are large enough to read on a phone. The layout guard asserts every foreground box inside SAFE.
- Every text on a plate or sticker passes a measured contrast check (4.5:1 small, 3:1 large; not-yet-spoken caption words at least 3:1): render the stills twice (tools/phrase_stills.mjs, then again with --notext) and run `python3 tools/contrast_audit.py <stills> <notext stills>`. No light text on a light plate: deep plum or navy on mint, lemon and coral plates, white only on dark enough fills with a dark outline. The Abacus place chips and labels take `chipInk` / `labelInk` for this.
- BACKGROUND IS CALM AND SMOOTH, NOT FULL (user rule, 2026-10-06, set while building E15). The world is a soft gradient with
  gentle light and a few faint wide sunbeams. Detail (plants, coral, rocks, fish, bubbles) lives ONLY at the far left and
  right edges and along the floor, as a soft low-contrast frame that fades toward the middle, drawn slightly blurred (depth
  of field). The lesson area (behind the abacus, the cards and the caption) is open, clean water or sky: no dense plants, no
  hard dark vertical stripes through the middle, only a handful of slow fish and bubbles kept in the margins. Motion is slow
  sway only, no flicker, pleasant for 5 minutes. Ground pieces like a character's water band fade out sideways and downward;
  they must never read as a box. Check with a before/after sheet of the same 8 frames; re-run the contrast audit
  (`python3 tools/contrast_audit.py A B --landscape` for 16:9) on the calmer background.
- Never show an EMPTY card: a chip, row or step that has not arrived yet shows a dashed "?" ghost, or the card stays hidden until
  its first item (E15's `Ghosted`, `contentAt`).
- CAST RULES (2026-10-07): Pip = me in every episode; Fifi (small friend) only in small friend and family episodes; Tenzo (big friend) only in big friend and family episodes; name tags ME / SMALL FRIEND / BIG FRIEND; badges show only "5" and "10". See docs/COVE_SERIES_PLAN.md.
- INTERNAL OVERFLOW (2026-10-07): every card root calls `useOverflowGuard` (src/lib/overflowGuard.ts): no child or text may cross its card edge. The box guard alone cannot see a chip sticking out of its own card. Run the stills sweep AND an every-3rd-frame render, then LOOK at full-size frames (contact sheets hide clipping).
- The sum card sits in ONE fixed place in every phrase of an episode. Resolve hand collisions by moving the hand tag, never the card.

## Cast + formula rules added 12 Oct (from the E07 review)
- NO FRIEND BEFORE THE INTRO: the friend (Fifi / Tenzo), their name tag and number crown stay off stage until the narration first introduces the idea of a friend (E07: p19 "Every small number has a friend"). Only Pip ("ME") is on stage before that. Applies to every formula episode.
- The crown "5" / "10": the number sits fully inside its own plate (nothing clipped), the crown is seated on the head, and it drops on after the friend arrives.
- FORMULA ORDER IS FRIEND FIRST in the script, the audio AND the visuals: +3 = −2 +5, never "+5 −2" / "add five and take away two". Check every line that says a formula aloud.
- While the beads DO the formula, show it on screen (FormulaStrip) with the move being made lit: first the "−n" part, then the "+5" part.
- Finger "thumb" / "index finger" pills sit OUTSIDE the hand (above for an upward move, below for a downward one), never on top of the finger image.
- Cutting recorded audio: cut on the real gaps in the waveform (rms scan), NOT on the aligner's word times (they are loose); re-check by independent ASR and a click scan.
