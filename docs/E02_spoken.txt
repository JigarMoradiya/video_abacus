# --- 1 · HOOK ---
Last time we met the abacus.
Today we're going to make numbers with it.
Every number from zero all the way to nine.
And we only need one rod.

# --- 2 · ZERO ---
Let's start with nothing at all. Zero.
Zero means no beads are touching the beam.
Push them all away, the rod says zero.

# --- 3 · ONE TO FOUR ---
Now push one lower bead up to the beam.
That is one.
Push one more. One, two.
That is two.
One more again. One, two, three.
That is three.
One more. One, two, three, four.
That is four.

# --- 4 · THE WALL ---
Now let's make five.
Push another lower bead up. Oh, there aren't any left.
Four is as high as the lower beads can go.
So how do we make five?

# --- 5 · THE UPPER BEAD IS FIVE ---
Look above the beam. The upper bead.
It has been waiting there the whole time.
Send the lower beads back down.
Now bring the upper bead down with your index finger.
One upper bead, the rod says five.
That one little bead is worth five all by itself.

# --- 6 · SIX TO NINE ---
Now five is easy to build on.
Five and one lower bead, that's six.
Five and two lower beads, seven.
Five and three, eight.
Five and four, nine.
Nine is the biggest number one rod can show.

# --- 7 · THE READING RULE ---
Reading a rod is always the same.
First, is the upper bead down? If it is, that's five.
Then count the lower beads touching the beam.
Add them together, that's your number.
