# E03 · Adding two numbers — narration, one continuous take.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored by the aligner. Do not read them.
#
# Warm, playful, unhurried. Short sentences — let each one land before the next.
# Numbers are WORDS on purpose: the aligner matches them to the digits Whisper writes.

# --- 1 · HOOK ---
Last time we made every number from zero to nine.
Today we put two numbers together.
That is called adding.
And the abacus makes it easy.

# --- 2 · WHAT ADDING LOOKS LIKE ---
Adding just means moving more beads to the beam.
Start with your first number.
Then push up as many beads as you need.
The rod shows the answer all by itself.

# --- 3 · ONE PLUS TWO ---
# Count the run clearly and evenly — each number lands on its own bead.
Let's try one plus two.
First, push one lower bead up.
That is one.
Now push two more up.
One, two.
That is three. One plus two is three.

# --- 4 · TWO PLUS TWO ---
Now let's try two plus two.
Start with two beads up.
Push two more up.
Now four beads are touching the beam.
Two plus two is four.

# --- 5 · THE LOWER-BEAD RULE ---
Every lower bead you push up adds one.
But there are only four of them.
So the lower beads alone can make one, two, three or four.

# --- 6 · THE UPPER BEAD ADDS FIVE ---
To add five, we use the upper bead.
Start with nothing.
Push the upper bead down.
That is five.
One bead, one move.

# --- 7 · FIVE PLUS ONE, FIVE PLUS FOUR ---
Now five plus one.
The upper bead is already down.
Push one lower bead up.
Five plus one is six.
And five plus four.
Push all four lower beads up.
Five plus four is nine.

# --- 8 · YOUR TURN ---
# HOLD THREE FULL SECONDS OF SILENCE after the next line. Do not cut it short.
Your turn. Five plus two.
Push the upper bead down, then two lower beads up.
That is seven.
Nice work.

# --- 9 · WHEN THE BEADS RUN OUT ---
# Curious, not worried. This is a door being opened, not a problem.
One more thing.
Sometimes there is no room left for more lower beads.
That is a trick for next time.
