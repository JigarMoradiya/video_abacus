# --- 1 · HOOK ---
Last time we learned to make every number from zero to nine.
Today we are going to put two numbers together.
When we put two numbers together, that is called adding.
And the abacus makes adding really easy.

# --- 2 · WHAT ADDING MEANS ---
Adding on the abacus just means moving more beads to the beam.
First you make your starting number.
Then you push up as many more beads as you want to add.
When you stop, the rod is already showing you the answer.

# --- 3 · ONE PLUS TWO ---
Let us try one plus two together.
To make one, push one lower bead up to the beam.
The rod is showing one.
Now we add two, so push two more lower beads up.
Count them as they go up: one, two.
Three lower beads are touching the beam, so one plus two is three.

# --- 4 · TWO PLUS TWO ---
Now let us try two plus two.
To make two, push two lower beads up to the beam.
Now we add two more, so push two more lower beads up.
All four lower beads are touching the beam now.
So two plus two is four.

# --- 5 · THE LOWER BEAD RULE ---
Every lower bead you push up adds one more.
But each rod only has four lower beads.
So the lower beads on their own can make one, two, three or four.

# --- 6 · THE UPPER BEAD ADDS FIVE ---
When we want to add five, we use the upper bead instead.
Start with all the beads away from the beam, so the rod is showing zero.
Now push the upper bead down until it touches the beam.
The rod is showing five.
One single bead gave us five, in one move.

# --- 7 · FIVE PLUS ONE ---
Now let us try five plus one.
First we make five by pushing the upper bead down.
Then we add one, so push one lower bead up.
The upper bead is worth five and the lower bead is worth one.
So five plus one is six.

# --- 8 · FIVE PLUS FOUR ---
Let us try five plus four.
Make five again by pushing the upper bead down.
Now push all four lower beads up to the beam.
Five and four more makes nine.

# --- 9 · STARTING FROM ANY NUMBER ---
# The point of this section: your first number does not have to be five.
This works when you start from any number, not only from five.
Let us start from six this time.
To make six, push the upper bead down for five, and push one lower bead up for one.
The rod is showing six.
Now we add three, so push three more lower beads up.
Six plus three is nine.
Let us do one more, starting from seven.
To make seven, push the upper bead down, then push two lower beads up.
Now we add one, so push one more lower bead up.
Seven plus one is eight.

# --- 10 · YOUR TURN ---
# HOLD THREE FULL SECONDS OF SILENCE after the next line. Do not cut it short.
Now it is your turn. Try five plus two on your own abacus.
Push the upper bead down to make five, then push two lower beads up.
The rod is showing seven, so five plus two is seven.
That was lovely work.

# --- 11 · WHEN THE BEADS RUN OUT ---
# Curious, not worried. This is a door being opened, not a problem.
Here is one last thing to notice.
Make one on your rod, and then try to add four more.
There are only three lower beads left, so there is not enough room.
There is a clever trick for moments like that, and we will learn it very soon.
