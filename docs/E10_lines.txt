# ═══ FORMAT ═══ Not a teaching episode — a RECALL episode. E08 taught all four Little Friends
# for addition; E09 taught all four for subtraction. This is the game show that makes the child
# prove they remember all eight, mixed, not recited back in the same block order they were taught.
#
# ═══ VOICE ═══ Same complete-sentence rule as E08/E09, but faster and lighter — a game-show host,
# not a teacher. Short reactions are still full sentences ("Look at that, we are stuck." not
# "Stuck!"), but every round moves quickly: state the sum, hit the wall, name the friend, answer.
# No re-teaching of WHY the trick works — that was E08 and E09's job. This one is pure recall.
#
# ═══ THE EIGHT ROUNDS, LOCKED ORDER ═══ mixed, not blocked — each of 1-4 appears once as
# addition and once as subtraction, split evenly across both halves:
#
#   R1  +1   four plus one            = five           (1's friend is 4)
#   R2  −3   six take away three      = three           (3's friend is 2)
#   R3  +4   one plus four            = five           (4's friend is 1)
#   R4  −2   six take away two        = four           (2's friend is 3)
#   --- like ask ---
#   R5  −1   five take away one       = four           (1's friend is 4)
#   R6  +3   two plus three           = five           (3's friend is 2)
#   R7  −4   seven take away four     = three           (4's friend is 1)
#   R8  +2   three plus two           = five           (2's friend is 3)
#
# Every example is a real E08/E09 worked case, reused on purpose — recall works better against a
# sum the child has already met than a brand new one.
#
# ═══ LIKE ASK ═══ new spot, new wording, per EPISODE_RULES §3.9a: after round 4, on the halfway
# win. Not the close, not 94-96%.


# --- 1 · GAME INTRO ---
Are you ready to play a game?
It is called Beat the Beads!
I will spin the wheel, and it will land on a number.
You tell me that number's friend, before I count to three.
If the beads get stuck, you will know exactly what to do.
Let's find out who remembers best.
