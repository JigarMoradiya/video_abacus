# Quick Addition on Rod 1 — recording script (9:16 short reel, ~45-50s)
# Lines starting with # are notes and are NOT read aloud. Everything else IS read aloud,
# exactly as written.
#
# FOURTH of the foundational reels (after Bead Values, Which Finger?, Count to 10 — before
# the two already-built Math Heroes reels). ADDITION ONLY. Four examples, all genuinely
# "it just fits" — no complement/friend trick anywhere. 4+1 was cut from the first draft:
# that's literally one of Math Heroes reel 1's own missions (friend of 1 is 4), so it
# belongs to the trick reel, not this one. Every complement pair (1&4, 2&3) is avoided here
# on purpose.
#
#   1 + 2 = 3   lower beads only
#   2 + 2 = 4   lower beads only
#   2 + 5 = 7   the upper bead alone — always worth 5, untouched by the lower beads
#   6 + 3 = 9   the upper bead is already down (6 = upper + 1 lower); the lower beads still
#               have exactly enough room (1 + 3 = 4) for the add to be just as direct
#
# Between each example the rod resets to empty before the next one starts — the short
# transition line ("Let's try another!" etc.) is the beat where that reset happens on
# screen, so the cut to the next example never feels abrupt.

Adding on one rod is super quick — check out these examples!
One bead is up. Push two more up — one plus two is three!
Let's try another!
Two beads are up. Push two more up — two plus two is four!
Next one!
Two beads are up. Bring the upper bead down — two plus five is seven!
One more!
Six is up — one lower bead, plus the upper bead. Add three more lower beads — six plus three is nine!
Here's one tip: lower beads always move first, then the upper bead!
Lower beads or the upper bead — adding is always quick!
