# --- 3 · THE WALL ---
Now let us try ten take away one.
Make ten. One bead on the tens rod, and nothing on the ones rod.
Look at the ones rod. How many beads can we take away?
# ⏸ hold 2s
None! The ones rod is empty.

# --- 4 · THE BIG FRIEND HELPS: 10 − 1 ---
Do not worry. A big friend can help us.
We want to take away one.
Who is one's friend?
# ⏸ hold 2s
Nine! One and nine make ten.

# THE FORMULA COMES FIRST — first taking-away episode, so the rule is taught and explained
# before a single bead moves (user call). The formula strip appears on "Minus one equals plus
# nine, minus ten" and stays up through the bead work.
So here is the big friend trick for taking away.
Take away one is the same as add nine, then take away ten.
Minus one equals plus nine, minus ten.
Why does it work?
Taking away ten takes away too much. It takes away nine extra.
So we add the nine back first. And we are left with taking away one.
Let us do it on the abacus.

Remember our rule. The ones rod goes first, and lower beads move before the upper bead.
First, add nine on the ones rod. Push four lower beads up, then the upper bead down.
Now the tens rod gets its turn. Take away ten. Take one bead away from the tens rod.
And there it is. Nine!
Ten take away one is nine.
Plus nine, minus ten. That is our first big friend formula for taking away.


# --- 5 · NOT EMPTY, STILL STUCK: 12 − 3 ---
Let us try twelve take away three.
Make twelve. One bead on the tens rod, and two lower beads on the ones rod.
Can we take away three from the ones rod?
# ⏸ hold 2s
No! There are only two beads there.
So we call a big friend. Three's friend is seven.
Minus three equals plus seven, minus ten.
Ones rod first. Add seven. Push two lower beads up, then the upper bead down.
Now take one bead away from the tens rod.
Twelve take away three is nine!

# --- 6 · A BIG NUMBER: 11 − 7 ---
Now, what about taking away a big number, like seven?
Let us try eleven take away seven.
Make eleven. One bead on the tens rod, and one lower bead on the ones rod.
Can we take away seven from just one bead?
# ⏸ hold 2s
No way!
Seven's friend is three.
Minus seven equals plus three, minus ten.
Ones rod first. Push three lower beads up.
Then take one bead away from the tens rod.
Eleven take away seven is four!
The big numbers work the same way.


# --- 7 · THE PATTERN ---
Now stop and look. Did you see it?
Every single time, we did the same two things.
Add the friend on the ones rod first.
Then take one bead away from the tens rod.
The friend changes.
But ten never does.
There is one for every number, from take away one to take away nine.
That is nine big friend formulas for taking away.
