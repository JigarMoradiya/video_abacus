# THIRD of the foundational reels (after Bead Values, after Which Finger?, before quick
# add/subtract on rod 1). Real abacus sequence, single rod: 1-4 are lower beads one at a
# time; 5 is the lower beads cleared and the upper bead alone; 6-9 build back up on top of
# the upper bead; 9 is the most one rod can hold; 10 carries into the tens rod.

Let's count all the way to ten on the abacus!
One, two, three, four — four lower beads, one at a time!
Now clear them and drop the upper bead — that's five!
Six, seven, eight, nine — add a lower bead each time!
Nine is the most this one rod can show!
For ten, we move over — one ten, and zero ones!
You just counted one to ten on the abacus!
