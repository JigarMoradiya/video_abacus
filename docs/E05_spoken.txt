# --- 1 · HOOK ---
Last time, we learned to make big numbers using more than one rod.
Today, we're going back to one rod to learn something new.
We're going to take numbers away.

# --- 2 · WHAT TAKING AWAY MEANS ---
Taking away is the opposite of adding.
When we added, we pushed beads towards the beam.
So when we take away, we push beads away from the beam.
That's the whole idea.
The rest is just practice.

# --- 3 · THE FINGER CHANGES ---
There's one more thing that changes, your finger.
When we add, the thumb pushes the lower beads up.
When we take away, your index finger pushes them back down.

# --- 4 · FOUR TAKE AWAY TWO ---
Let's try four, take away two.
First, make four.
Push four lower beads up to the beam.
The rod is showing four.
Now take away two.
Push two lower beads back down.
Two beads have moved away from the beam.
Two lower beads are still touching the beam.
So four, take away two, is two.

# --- 5 · EIGHT TAKE AWAY THREE ---
Now let's try eight, take away three.
First, make eight.
Push the upper bead down for five.
Then push three lower beads up for three.
Five and three is eight.
Now take away three.
Push three lower beads back down.
The upper bead doesn't move at all.
Only the lower beads move away from the beam.
The upper bead is still worth five.
So eight, take away three, is five.

# --- 6 · THE RULE SO FAR ---
Here's the rule so far.
If you only need to move lower beads, just push them away from the beam.

# --- 7 · FIVE TAKE AWAY FIVE ---
Now what if we need to take away five?
Five lives on the upper bead.
So the upper bead has to move.
Let's try five, take away five.
First, make five.
Push the upper bead down.
The rod is showing five.
Now take away five.
Push the upper bead back up.
Every bead is away from the beam.
So five, take away five, is zero.

# --- 8 · SEVEN TAKE AWAY FIVE ---
Let's try seven, take away five.
First, make seven.
Push the upper bead down for five.
Then push two lower beads up for two.
Now take away five.
Push the upper bead back up.
The two lower beads don't move.
They're still touching the beam.
So seven, take away five, is two.

# --- 9 · NINE TAKE AWAY EIGHT ---
Sometimes both kinds of beads have to move.
Let's try nine, take away eight.
First, make nine.
Push the upper bead down and all four lower beads up.
Now take away eight.
Eight is five and three.
So we move both.
First, push three lower beads down.
Then push the upper bead up.
One lower bead is still touching the beam.
So nine, take away eight is one.

# --- 10 · YOUR TURN ---
Now it's your turn.
Make nine on your own abacus and take away four.
What number is left?
Nine, take away four is five.
Great job.
