# E04 · Bigger numbers — narration, one continuous take.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored by the aligner. Do not read them.
#
# Warm, unhurried. This episode is a READING lesson, not an arithmetic one: nothing is added
# or taken away, numbers are only SET and READ. Let each number land before moving on.
#
# Source: the app's Level 1 Lesson 4 (Place Value) and Lesson 5 (Numbers to 99). The worked
# numbers — ten, twenty-three, fifty-six, ninety-nine, thirty-eight — are the app's own.
#
# Numbers are WORDS on purpose: the aligner matches them to the digits Whisper writes.

# ⏸ HOLD THREE SECONDS after "What number is that?" — the child has to be given time to work

# --- 1 · HOOK ---
Last time, we added two numbers on one rod.
But one rod can only show up to nine.
So what do we do when a number is bigger than nine?
That is what the other rods are for.

# --- 2 · WHICH ROD IS WHICH ---
On an abacus this size, the ones rod is the one on the far right.
On a much bigger abacus, the ones rod sits in the middle instead — we saw that in the first video.
Now look at the rod just to the left of the ones rod.
That one is the tens rod.
Every bead on the tens rod is worth ten, not one.

# --- 3 · MAKING TEN ---
Let's make ten.
Push one lower bead up on the tens rod.
Leave the ones rod at zero.
One bead on the tens rod is one ten, and one ten is ten.

# --- 4 · TWENTY-THREE ---
Now let's make twenty-three.
On the tens rod, push two lower beads up.
Two beads on the tens rod is two tens, which is twenty.
Now, on the ones rod, push three lower beads up.
That is three ones.
Twenty and three is twenty-three.

# --- 5 · THE READING RULE ---
Here is how to read any number on two rods.
Read the tens rod first, then read the ones rod.

# --- 6 · FIFTY-SIX ---
Let's try fifty-six.
On the tens rod, push the upper bead down.
The upper bead on the tens rod is worth five tens, which is fifty.
On the ones rod, push the upper bead down for five.
Then push one lower bead up for one.
That makes six ones.
Fifty and six is fifty-six.

# --- 7 · NINETY-NINE, THE MAXIMUM ---
What is the biggest number that two rods can show?
On the tens rod, push the upper bead down and all four lower beads up.
That is nine tens, which is ninety.
Now do exactly the same on the ones rod.
That is nine ones.
Ninety and nine is ninety-nine.
Ninety-nine is the most that two rods can show.
So with two rods, you can make every number from zero to ninety-nine.
That is one hundred different numbers.

# --- 8 · YOUR TURN ---
Now it is your turn.
The tens rod shows three, and the ones rod shows eight.
What number is that?
#   ⏸ HOLD THREE SECONDS HERE
Three tens is thirty, and eight ones is eight.
Thirty and eight is thirty-eight.
Great job.

# --- 9 · ONE MORE ROD ---
And it does not stop at two rods.
The next rod to the left is the hundreds rod.
Every bead on that rod is worth one hundred.
Two beads on the hundreds rod, four on the tens rod, and seven on the ones rod.
Two hundred, forty and seven is two hundred and forty-seven.
Every time you move one rod to the left, each bead is worth ten times more.

# --- 10 · CLOSE ---
Now, try making some big numbers on your own abacus.
