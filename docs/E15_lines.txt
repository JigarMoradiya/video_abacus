#  1. WHICH DOC WAS WRONG: docs/EPISODE_ROADMAP.md. It labels E15 "Family Plus (+5−10)" and E16
#     "Family Minus (−5+10)" — the signs are SWAPPED. The app (iOS Level2FormulaView.swift:
#     "Family + (−5+10)" / "Family − (+5−10)"; Level2Content.swift chapter 10; Android
#     Level2FormulaScreen.kt: the same) and the reference_abacus_formulas memory both say Family PLUS
#     is "−5 +10" (heaven bead up, carry a ten) and Family MINUS is "+5 −10". The roadmap's labels
#     read like iOS/APP_FEATURES.md line 78 ("+5−10 and −5+10 combined"), which lists the two in the
#     other order and is ambiguous. The math settles it: adding must net a positive number, and
#     −5+10 is +5; +5−10 is −5. E15 = "Family +" = −5+10. E16 = "Family −" = +5−10. (Not edited —
#     flagged for the roadmap to be corrected.)
#
#  2. NOTATION / BEAD ORDER. Series convention (E11–E13): ones rod first, LOWER beads before the
#     UPPER bead, tens rod last. The APP already writes Family + in exactly that order:
#         +6 = +1 −5 +10   (add lower beads, push the upper bead away, carry a ten)
#     The memory file writes the same moves in a different ORDER ("+6 = −5+10+1", heaven first, add
#     the earth bead last). That ordering breaks the lower-beads-first rule, so E15 uses the APP's
#     order: "Plus six equals plus one, minus five, plus ten." Spoken the way E13 speaks its
#     formula ("Minus three equals plus seven, minus ten."). The memory file's bead order should be
#     corrected to match.
#
#  3. WHY IT EXISTS (the derivation the script teaches before any bead moves). Big friend adding is
#     +n = −friend +10, and its first step "take away the friend" needs enough LOWER beads. When it
#     does not have them, that step is itself a SMALL-friend taking-away (E09): −friend is
#     +(n−5) −5.  So Family = big friend + small friend:
#         +6 = −4 +10, and −4 = +1 −5      →  +6 = +1 −5 +10
#         +7 = −3 +10, and −3 = +2 −5      →  +7 = +2 −5 +10
#         +8 = −2 +10, and −2 = +3 −5      →  +8 = +3 −5 +10
#         +9 = −1 +10, and −1 = +4 −5      →  +9 = +4 −5 +10
#     The lower beads added are always n − 5.
#
#  4. WHEN FAMILY IS NEEDED (exact, brute-forced over every ones digit d 0–9 for n = 1…9, checking
#     whether "take away the friend f = 10−n from d" is a plain move; trigger for any trick at all
#     is d + n > 9). Family is needed when the heaven bead is down (d ≥ 5) and the friend f is
#     greater than the lower beads showing (d − 5):
#         formula   friend   FAMILY when ones digit d is      pure big friend works when d is
#         +6        4        5, 6, 7, 8                        4, 9
#         +7        3        5, 6, 7                           3, 4, 8, 9
#         +8        2        5, 6                              2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9
#         +9        1        5                                 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9
#         +1…+5     —        NEVER (friend ≥ 5 comes off with the upper bead; or d too low)
#     That is 10 (d, n) pairs — exactly the app's familyAddPairs() (iOS Level2Content.swift:237).
#     Four Family formulas for adding: +6, +7, +8, +9. In kid words: "the big friend gets stuck —
#     there are not enough lower beads to take the friend away."
#
#  5. CONTENT FLAG FOUND ON THE WAY (not part of this script, tell whoever owns the app copy):

#     example rows belong in Family, not Big Friend. E12 and E14's examples are unaffected: every
#     one was already checked (E14: 29+6 has d = 9, plain big friend).

# --- 1 · RECAP HOOK — pays E14's teaser ---
Hello again!
Last time, in Beat the Beads, you remembered all eighteen formulas.
I promised you a brand new kind of trick. One that adds and takes away, both at once.
# ⏸ hold 1s
Today, it is here. It helps when the big friend gets stuck.
# ⏸ hold 1s

# --- 2 · THE PROBLEM FIRST, THEN THE NAME — need before name ---
A reminder: the big friend takes the friend off the ones rod, then carries one bead to the tens rod.
You tell me. What is six's big friend?
# ⏸ hold 3s
Four!
But here is a problem. Sometimes the ones rod cannot give the friend away.
There are not enough lower beads showing, and the upper bead is down.
Then the big friend alone gets stuck. We need something more.
# ⏸ hold 2s
Good news. The small friend can help. Four's small friend is one, and four and one make five.
The big friend and the small friend, working together. That is called the family.
# ⏸ hold 1s
So, look at the ones rod first. If the big friend gets stuck like that, call the family.
# ⏸ hold 2s

# --- 3 · THE WALL — the proof: 7 + 6 ---
Let us see it. Seven plus six.
Make seven on the ones rod. Push two lower beads up with your thumb.
# ⏸ hold 1s
Then push the upper bead down with your index finger.
Remember: up is the thumb, and down is the index finger.
# ⏸ hold 2s
Six's friend is four, so take away four.
How many lower beads can we take away?
# ⏸ hold 3s
Only two!
We need four. The big friend is stuck, just like we said.
# ⏸ hold 2s

# --- 4 · THE FAMILY FORMULA FIRST, THEN THE BEADS: 7 + 6 ---
Plus six equals minus four, plus ten.
But the small friend can take away four. Add one, then take away five.
Minus four equals plus one, minus five.
# ⏸ hold 2s
Put them together. Here is the family trick for adding.
Plus six equals plus one, minus five, plus ten.
# ⏸ hold 3s
Why? Plus ten adds four too many, so we take four away.
# ⏸ hold 1s
Remember our rule. Ones rod first, and lower beads before the upper bead.
Plus one. Push one lower bead up. Thumb!
# ⏸ hold 2s
Minus five. Push the upper bead up. Thumb again!
# ⏸ hold 1s
Oh! Only three on the ones rod? Watch what happens next.
Now carry one bead to the tens rod.
And there it is. Seven plus six is thirteen!
That is our first family formula.
# ⏸ hold 2s

# --- THE LIKE ASK — MID-ROLL, RIGHT AFTER THE FIRST WIN ---
Having fun with the family?
Give this video a like, and subscribe to meet the rest.
# ⏸ hold 1s

# --- 5 · A BIGGER NUMBER: 26 + 7 ---
Bigger numbers now. Make twenty-six.
On the ones rod, one lower bead and the upper bead. Then two lower beads on the tens rod. Thumb up, index finger down!
Seven's friend is three. Can we take away three lower beads?
# ⏸ hold 3s
No! Only one.
Stuck again.
Call the family. Plus seven equals plus two, minus five, plus ten.
# ⏸ hold 3s
Push two lower beads up.
# ⏸ hold 1s
Now push the upper bead up.
# ⏸ hold 1s
Carry one bead to the tens rod. Thumb all the way!
Twenty-six plus seven is thirty-three!
# ⏸ hold 1s

# --- 7 · THE PATTERN ---
Look. Every time: plus some lower beads, minus five, plus ten.
Every move went up. So every move was a thumb.
# ⏸ hold 1s
Plus six, add one. Plus seven, add two. Plus eight, add three. Plus nine, add four.
But minus five, and plus ten, never change.
# ⏸ hold 3s
Four family formulas for adding: plus six, seven, eight and nine.
# ⏸ hold 1s

# --- 8 · YOUR TURN: 5 + 9 ---
Your turn. Make five on the ones rod. Just the upper bead. That is down, so use your index finger. Then add nine.
What is nine's friend?
# ⏸ hold 3s
One!
Look at the ones rod. There are no lower beads at all. So call the family.
Nine is how many more than five?
# ⏸ hold 3s
Four! Plus nine equals plus four, minus five, plus ten.
# ⏸ hold 2s
Now do all three moves. Ones rod first, and remember, up is the thumb.
# ⏸ hold 2s
What number is showing now?
# ⏸ hold 3s
Five plus nine is fourteen!
Well done! You did the family trick all by yourself.
# ⏸ hold 1s
