# --- 1 · THE HOOK: a rod with no beads left ---
We are about to get REALLY stuck.
No beads left to push — not one, anywhere on this rod.
So we carry a bead to the tens rod instead.
Let us try nine plus one.

# --- 2 · IT GOES WRONG ---
First, make nine.
Push four lower beads up on the ones rod.
Now push the upper bead down for five.
Four and five together is nine.
Now we want to add one more.
But look at the ones rod.
Every bead on it is already pushed.
There is nothing left to push.
So we cannot add one the easy way.

# --- 3 · CARRY TO THE TENS ROD ---
But we are not stuck.
There is another rod — the tens rod.
The tens rod is empty right now.
One bead on the tens rod is worth ten beads on the ones rod.
So when the ones rod is full, we carry a bead to the tens rod instead.

# --- 4 · BIG FRIENDS ---
Here is the clever part.
Some friends do not make five together.
They make ten together.
One and nine are friends.
Two and eight are friends.
Three and seven are friends.
Four and six are friends.
Five and five are friends.
Say them with me.
One and nine.
Two and eight.
Three and seven.
Four and six.
Five and five.
These are called the big friends.

# --- 5 · THE TRICK ---
Now watch what the friends do.
We wanted to add one.
One's friend is nine.
So instead of adding one here, we take away nine here, and carry one bead to the tens rod.
Because taking away nine, then adding ten, is the same as adding one.
It is the same answer, the easy way round.

# --- 6 · THE TRICK ON THE ABACUS ---
Let us do it.
Our ones rod is showing nine.
First, we take away nine.
Push the four lower beads down, then push the upper bead up.
The ones rod now shows zero.
Now, we carry.
Push one lower bead up on the tens rod.
That bead is worth ten.
The tens rod shows one.
Nine plus one is ten.
We did it, and we never ran out of beads.

# --- 7 · ONE MORE ---
Let us try eight plus two.
First, make eight.
Push three lower beads up, then the upper bead down.
Now add two.
Two's friend is eight.
So first, take away eight from the ones rod.
The ones rod shows zero.
Then carry one bead to the tens rod.
The tens rod shows one.
Eight plus two is ten.

# --- 8 · YOUR TURN ---
Now it is your turn.
Make seven on your own abacus.
Then add three.
Three's friend is seven.
So take away seven from the ones rod, then carry one bead to the tens rod.
What number is showing now?
Seven plus three is ten.
Well done.
