# Bead Values — recording script (9:16 short reel, ~40-45s)
# Lines starting with # are notes and are NOT read aloud. Everything else IS read aloud,
# exactly as written. Read at a quick, energetic pace — this is a short-form hook.
#
# THE FIRST of the foundational reels (before Math Heroes 1 & 2): what a bead is actually
# worth, on any rod. Hosted by the app's own mascots, Bo (blue) and Pip (purple) — no
# superhero framing here, this is direct teaching.

Every bead has a secret power — let's find out!

Each rod on the abacus has two kinds of beads.

These lower beads — each one is worth just one.

But this upper bead — worth five, all by itself!

Now the tens rod: lower beads worth ten each, upper bead worth fifty!

And the hundreds rod: lower beads worth a hundred, upper worth five hundred!

Ones, tens, hundreds — bigger and bigger, rod by rod!

Learn it all with Abacus Kids — free on the App Store and Google Play!
